How to complete a score
  1. Score every Part A item 0–max using the front-side anchors. Whole numbers only — interpolate between the 0 and Max descriptions.
  2. Sum item scores within each domain.
  3. Cap Domain 5's contribution at 16 (its items can raw-sum to 18).
  4. Sum all 7 domain contributions for the Part A Total (0–100), and look up the Tier below.
  5. For Part B: count current participants by status, pick this month's phase condition, and compute Monthly WU using the formula below.
  6. Two independent scorers should complete this separately and reconcile — see the user guide.

Known ambiguities & interpretive calls
  • Domain 5 cap. Items sum to 18; the domain contributes at most 16 to the 100-pt total — this is why the 7 domain maxima sum to exactly 100.
  • Phase multiplier scope. Applies to the protocol total (Static + participant WU), not just the participant term — the source table's caption says "apply to the protocol total."
  • Multiplier stacking is undefined if two conditions land in the same month. Apply exactly one; note your reasoning.
  • FTE figure (if calculated) is for this protocol only — not a staffing determination. Portfolio FTE needs role-separated WU + a concurrency penalty (see rubric).
