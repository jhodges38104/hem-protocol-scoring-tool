Capacity constant & FTE (optional)

The rubric explicitly warns against importing an external ratio — the published IWAT figure of 500–600 WU/month is on a different scale and is not meant to be reused here. Your site derives its own constant C (WU one 1.0 FTE coordinator sustains per month) from the Part E time study. Leave blank until that calibration exists.

Any figure shown here is for this one protocol only — it is not a staffing determination. Actual FTE needs require portfolio-wide WU summed separately by role (a regulatory coordinator's WU and a bedside CRC's WU are not substitutable) plus a concurrency penalty (×1.1 at 6–8 concurrent protocols per coordinator, ×1.2 at 9+), applied at the portfolio level — see the Part B corrections in the source rubric.
